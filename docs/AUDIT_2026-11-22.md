# PerpPrime — Technical Audit and Fixes (2026-11-22)

An external review of the v1.0.0 source found one critical backtest bug and
four live-risk gaps. This document records what was verified, what was
fixed, and what is still open.

## Verdict

**No live trading.** The corrected backtest shows no edge, so paper trading
only. The P1 execution gaps are closed; the remaining items are research and
modelling work (P2/P3).

## P0 — Backtest entry look-ahead (fixed)

**Finding.** The engine computed the signal from a closed bar `i` but filled
at bar `i`'s open:

```python
entry = slip(o, side, entering=True)            # o = signal bar open
pos = Position(entry_ts=rows[i + 1][0], ...)    # timestamp = next bar
```

The signal only exists once bar `i` closes (`c > EMA20`), so the fill price
was from before the signal. Because the signal bar is directional by
construction, every trade received roughly one bar body of free profit.

**Impact.** The one-line fix reverses the published results:

| Period | Symbol | v1.0.0 | Corrected |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 12 months | BTC | +32.54% | **-0.13%** |
| 12 months | ETH | +42.30% | **-5.44%** |
| 12 months | SOL | +42.06% | **-10.77%** |
| Bull 2023 | BTC | +20.01% | **-6.45%** |
| Bull 2024 | BTC | +31.37% | **-9.31%** |
| Correction 2024 | BTC | +12.43% | **-8.96%** |

12 of 15 one-hour period/symbol cells are negative after the fix. The full
table and the retraction notice are in [BACKTEST.md](BACKTEST.md).

**Fix.** `entry = slip(rows[i + 1][1], side, entering=True)`.

**Regression test.**
`tests/test_backtest.py::test_entry_fills_at_next_bar_open_not_signal_bar_open`
builds a bar where the next open differs from the signal open and asserts the
trade uses the next open.

## P1 — Risk and execution (fixed)

### 1. Equity ignored unrealised PnL

`CcxtExchange.balance()` returned ccxt's `total`, which Bybit maps to
`walletBalance` — open losses were invisible to the daily-loss and drawdown
gates.

**Fix:** prefer the venue's `totalEquity` (UNIFIED) or `equity`
(CONTRACT/SPOT), fall back to the parsed total.
**Test:** `tests/test_exchange.py::test_balance_includes_unrealised_pnl`.

### 2. Unknown spread allowed trading

`evaluate_gates` returned `ALLOW` when `spread_bps` was `None`, so a failed
order-book read could not stop an order.

**Fix:** `None` now returns `BLOCK`. `PaperExchange` reports a measured
`0.0` spread so simulations still run.
**Test:** `tests/test_risk.py::test_missing_spread_blocks`.

### 3. Partial entry left a live residual

A partially filled market entry was bracketed for the filled size, but the
unfilled remainder was never cancelled and could fill later unprotected.

**Fix:** cancel the residual entry, re-read the venue position, bracket the
venue size, and abort if the venue is flat.
**Test:** `tests/test_execution.py::test_partial_entry_cancels_residual_and_uses_venue_size`.

### 4. Stop/TP orders were trusted without a venue check

A create response was treated as proof of protection. A venue can return an
order id whose status is already `rejected`, `canceled` or `expired`; the
position would then sit open with no live stop.

**Fix:** every bracket leg is confirmed after creation. `open` / `new` /
`untriggered` / `triggered` / `active` / `partially_filled` count as active;
`canceled` / `rejected` / `expired` / `failed` / `deactivated` count as dead;
an unknown status is re-read up to three times. A dead stop or TP raises, and
the existing handler emergency-closes the position.
**Tests:**
`test_rejected_stop_triggers_emergency_close`,
`test_stop_cancelled_after_poll_triggers_emergency_close`,
`test_rejected_take_profit_triggers_emergency_close`.
